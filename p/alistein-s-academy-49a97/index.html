Questions parents ask

Can my child join in the middle of the year?

Yes. We test the current level in the first week and give catch-up worksheets so your child can follow the class.

Do you offer a trial class?

The first class is free. If your child does not want to continue, you pay nothing.

What are the class timings?

Monday to Saturday, 2:30 PM to 9:00 PM. Each batch gets a fixed slot so it fits around school.

How do we pay?

Fees are paid monthly by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. You receive a receipt on WhatsApp.
